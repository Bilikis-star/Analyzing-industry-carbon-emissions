Analyzing Industry Carbon Emissions

Welcome to my data analytics project exploring greenhouse gas emissions and environmental performance metrics across corporate sectors.

Project Overview

This project uses SQL queries to analyze product carbon footprints, identify top-emitting industry sectors, and evaluate corporate ESG performance metrics.

Key Insights & Findings

  • Identified top emitting product groups and sector-specific carbon intensity trends.
  • Analyzed annual greenhouse gas contribution across global manufacturing and energy supply chains.
